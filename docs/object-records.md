# Cultural object records

The Listen feed includes a project-drafted cultural record titled “Luluwa (Lulua) people: art and history”. It is explicitly not written by a community member. The English summary follows the supplied project brief and links to the three cited Wikipedia pages. Those secondary sources are leads for further review, not community verification. The region is retained as supplied; the page requests confirmation of people/region relations and of present-day custodianship from a Luluwa contributor. French and Tshiluba fields are intentionally blank for speakers to fill.

The seed has no image. Add one only after checking the individual Wikimedia Commons file page and recording the exact source URL, author, licence, and licence URL in `docs/credits.md`. Do not infer a licence from the general Commons site.

Records marked “Restricted to a community” are reduced to title, region, access label, and the required community note for non-owners. Their image, full text, sources, and other fields are not rendered. The current seed is Public. A speaker story launched from a record is posted through the existing story flow as a separate story and retains its own author choices and server-created version fingerprint. The record/story association is held only in the current browser session in this proof of concept; it is not synced across browsers or stored as a new server relation.

Sources:

- [Wikipedia (fr): Luluwa (peuple)](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luluwa_(peuple)) — a stub article identifying Kasai-Occidental as an origin region.
- [Wikipedia (en): Luba art](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luba_art) — secondary overview of Luba visual/material culture and memory practices.
- [Wikipedia (en): Luba people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luba_people) — broader Luba history and culture overview.
